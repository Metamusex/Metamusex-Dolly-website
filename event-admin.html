MetaMuseX 3D Gallery

Exhibition admin

Set up the Artist's Exhibition: the immersive room shown first in every gallery.

🔑 Secret word

The secret word you wrote in the Apps Script (EVENT_ADMIN_KEY). It is only kept in this browser.

When ON (and between the two dates) the exhibition is the first room of every gallery. Before the opening it shows a countdown; after the closing date it disappears by itself.

🖼️ Artworks (up to 12)

OpenSea link: paste the link of the NFT page. Image on the site: upload the image to the event folder of the site on GitHub, then write its name, e.g. event/new-muse.jpg.

💬 Animated texts

One text per line (up to 10). In the Immersive Room they appear on the walls, alternating with the artworks. Leave it empty for images only.

✨ Animation

How the artworks fill the room. In every mode the transitions briefly mix different artworks.

🎵 Music (choose 2–3)

Only these songs play in the exhibition. Press ▶ to listen.

🔗 Link to announce

This link opens the exhibition alone — share it on Discord and X.

📊 Visitors

How many people opened the exhibition (each browser counts once; you and the preview don't count). Only you see these numbers.

—

🎁 Guestbook & prize draw

At the end of the show visitors can sign the guestbook with their wallet. The draw picks one at random among those who signed in the first 24 hours after the opening (one entry per wallet; your wallet doesn't count).

—

💬 Visitors' comments

What visitors wrote when they signed the guestbook (newest first).

🎯 Frame the artwork

Click the most important point (for example the eyes). The bright window shows what the walls of the Immersive Room will show.
