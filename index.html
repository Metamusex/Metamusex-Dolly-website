About the Family

MetaMuseX is a multichain, multi-collection NFT universe born from hand-drawn art: a muse and her male counterpart anchor an ecosystem of interacting collections, spanning generative PFPs and fully hand-crafted 1/1 pieces, spread across Polygon and Ethereum.

Alongside it lives the Dolly universe — its own multichain, multi-collection family built around Dolly, a hand-drawn character reinterpreted across generative farm-life PFPs and further collections on Polygon and Base.

Together, MetaMuseX and Dolly form one connected family: shared Discord perks, periodic airdrops, giveaways, and an ever-expanding roster of collections across chains.
