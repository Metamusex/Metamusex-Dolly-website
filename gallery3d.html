✨ How to enjoy it
🎧 Sound onHeadphones make it even better.
⛶ Full screenPress F — and dim the lights.
🧭 Look aroundUse ← → or drag with the mouse — the show is on every wall, and on the ceiling.
🚶 Move↑ ↓ to walk closer or step back.
🤫 Stay stillThe menus fade away and the room is all yours.

⚠️ Photosensitivity warning — this show contains flashing lights, glitch effects and fast-changing images, which may affect people with photosensitive epilepsy. If you feel unwell, stop watching.
